About these numbers. Hourly values are an illustrative simulation of a 1 MW / 2 MWh site with 90 % round-trip efficiency, not measurements. They will be replaced by MATLAB / Simulink results and pilot data.

Savings, payback, energy shifted and CO₂ figures are the modeled targets from the GridBESS EMS proposal. Emission factor: Republic of Turkey Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources, 2022. Demand charges at 30–70 % of commercial bills: NREL study reported by Utility Dive.
